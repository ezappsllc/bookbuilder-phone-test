    How it decides. It reads two prompts BookBuilder built from its sample book, with the co-writer's instructions swapped for stand-in text of the same length: a short scene (about 700 tokens) and a chapter with three characters' cards (about 1,500 tokens). Paragraphs means it starts writing the chapter prompt within 15 seconds and writes at least 12 tokens a second. Sentences means within 45 seconds and at least 4 tokens a second. A token is about three quarters of a word.

    Engines. GPU: WebLLM 0.2.87 over WebGPU. CPU: llama.cpp in WebAssembly (wllama 2.4.0). Models come from Hugging Face and stay in this browser until you delete them.
